

* NOAA Office For Coastal Management Sea Level Rise Inundation Data *


Metadata:
- Sea Level Rise Inundation:
	- https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/48105
	- https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/48106

- Mapping Confidence:
	- https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/48107


Data Types:
- Sea Level Rise Inundation Vector Polygon Data:
	- Ocean-connected sea level rise inundation for the amount specified, containing "_slr_" in the name.
	- Unconnected low lying areas that may flood, containing "_low_" in the name.
	- Provided in geopackages.
- Depth Rasters:
	- Ocean-connected sea level rise inundation, depth in meters above ground for the given sea level rise amount.
	- Provided as cloud optimized geotiffs (.tif).
- Mapping Confidence Rasters:
	- Represent areas of mapping confidence for the given sea level rise amount.
	- Provided as cloud optimized geotiffs (.tif).
